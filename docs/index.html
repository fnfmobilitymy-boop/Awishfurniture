04 / START WITH YOUR QUESTIONLook at the surface. Think about the feel.

What needs
a little care?

Every piece has a different story.
A clear description makes a better starting point.

01 / Surface & upholstery↗

The surface.

Show us the visible wear.

Torn upholstery, worn surfaces or local cracking? Show the area, extent and overall condition clearly.

02 / Comfort & support↗

The comfort.

Tell us how it feels.

Soft seating, sagging or uneven support? Explain where it happens and how you use the furniture.

03 / Structure & noises↗

The structure.

Look closer at the details.

Loose parts, tilting or noises? Describe the location and circumstances. Feasibility depends on the actual condition.

Not sure what is wrong? Start with an overall photo.
