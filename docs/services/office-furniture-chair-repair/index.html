GLOBAL MARS / Office buildings

An office enquiry starts with a clear inventory.

List the furniture types, quantities and affected areas. Tell us whether the concern is upholstery, seating comfort or visible structural damage, and include photographs. Any handling, access requirements, project timing and proposed work need to be discussed and confirmed rather than assumed.

What you can enquire about

  • Reception sofas and upholstered waiting-area seating
  • Meeting-room chairs and related furniture
  • Worn upholstery, cushions and support concerns
  • Coordinated restoration enquiries for multiple pieces

Before you get in touch

  • A furniture list and approximate quantities
  • Overall photos and representative close-ups
  • The part of the office where furniture is used
  • Access, timing and other project requirements

Common questions

Can I enquire about furniture across an office building?

Yes. Share an inventory, photographs and the affected areas. The type and extent of work should be confirmed for each furniture category.

Are every chair mechanism and material repairable?

Repair feasibility depends on the actual item and damage. Describe the mechanism or material issue and provide photos; do not assume every part or repair can be supplied.
