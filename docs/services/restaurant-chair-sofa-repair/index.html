Common questions

Can I enquire about restaurant dining chairs?

Yes. Send an overall photograph, details of the affected seats and the quantity involved. The condition and proposed work need to be reviewed before a quotation is confirmed.

What should I include for a booth-seating enquiry?

Include the overall arrangement, affected sections, approximate dimensions and whether the seating is fixed. Do not assume all installation or removal work is included; discuss the scope first.
