GLOBAL MARS / Furniture restoration

Start with the furniture you have.

A torn cover, a sagging seat and a loose joint are different problems. Share the overall condition, close-ups of the affected area and what you want to retain. We can then discuss the appropriate scope, materials, cost and next steps; the available work must be confirmed for the actual furniture.

What you can enquire about

  • Sofas, armchairs and upholstered seating
  • Worn, torn or damaged covers and upholstery
  • Seat comfort, cushions and support concerns
  • Visible frame, joint or structural issues

Before you get in touch

  • An overall front and side photograph
  • Clear close-ups of the damaged areas
  • Approximate size and number of pieces
  • Your preferred colour, material or details to retain

Common questions

Can an old sofa or chair be restored?

It depends on its structure, upholstery and the extent of damage. Send overall and close-up photographs first so the actual condition can be discussed.

Can you give a fixed price from the website?

The website does not give an unverified fixed price. The furniture condition, size, chosen material and agreed work need to be considered before a quotation is confirmed.
